Methodology. Compiled via targeted web research rather than a single live places API — always confirm hours and current status before visiting. The star rating shown and sorted on is Google's rating only, where a source explicitly cited a Google figure — Tripadvisor, Yelp, and The Infatuation scores use different scales and audiences and are not blended in, though the original text is preserved in each row's tooltip on hover. Places without a confirmed Google rating show as unrated (—) rather than a stand-in from another source. Review counts are not shown: they were sourced from third-party aggregators whose figures could not be verified against live data and were found to be unreliable, sometimes off by an order of magnitude. Fast food and coffee-only counters were excluded by design.
