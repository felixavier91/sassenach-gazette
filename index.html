Methodology. Compiled via targeted web research rather than a single live places API, so treat review counts as directional, not authoritative — always confirm hours and current status before visiting. The star rating shown and sorted on is Google's rating only (currently available for 1,345 of 1,723 places, where a source explicitly cited a Google figure) — Tripadvisor, Yelp, and The Infatuation scores use different scales and audiences and are not blended in, though the original text is preserved in each row's tooltip on hover. Places without a confirmed Google rating show as unrated (—) rather than a stand-in from another source. Fast food and coffee-only counters were excluded by design.
